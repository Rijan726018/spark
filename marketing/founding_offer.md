value first, ask second. Max 3 touches, each a different angle, never a bump.
Log every objection verbatim in `objections.md` — that log becomes the
Aug 4 launch copy.

> Hey [name] — I ran your channel through a tool I'm building and it turned

> Curious: how do you decide what to shoot each morning? Building this tool

> set your niche up with you on a 10-minute call: [payment link]

then ask plainly: "Founding is $79 for the year, locked forever — want me
