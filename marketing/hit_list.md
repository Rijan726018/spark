# Concierge hit list — 25–30 sub-50k creators

Wedge niches (per the ship plan's strategy panel): **personal finance** and
**faceless Shorts** primary, AI-news secondary. Sub-50k subs — big enough to
care about output, small enough to answer DMs.

## How to fill this list (30–45 min, do not skip the criteria)

1. YouTube search per niche seed ("budgeting for beginners", "dividend
   investing", "cash stuffing", "faceless channel", "reddit stories shorts",
   "AI news this week") → filter: **This month** + sort by view count →
   open channels of videos with high views but small channels.
2. For each candidate check: 5k–50k subs · uploaded within 14 days ·
   Shorts-active or daily-format · DMs open on X/IG (link usually in the
   channel About tab).
3. Once the YouTube key is live, Spark itself is the scout: run the niche,
   open the reference videos, check their channels — those creators are
   *already riding the signals Spark surfaces*. Best possible prospects.
4. One row per creator. The hook column is mandatory — a DM without a
   channel-specific hook is spam.
